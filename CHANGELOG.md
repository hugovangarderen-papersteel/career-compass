# Changelog

## 0.0.7 (not released yet)

Phase 5: the map and polish.

- **Map.** Every company with a known address on a map (MapLibre on OpenFreeMap tiles, with the
  OpenStreetMap credit). Each point is coloured by the road time already worked out for it:
  within your travel limit, further away, or not known yet. Your home address never reaches the app.
- **Companies grid.** All companies in one table with filters for size, sector, travel and type of
  role, and your fit, travel time and the role forms each company shows signs of.
- **My ranking.** Pick the companies you like most and put them in your own order. It is saved with
  your account and comes first in the company list.
- **Stories & media.** The news and media found while reading the companies' pages, newest first,
  each with its source.
- **Settings.** Text size in three steps and a high-contrast mode, saved with your account.
- **Your week is ready.** A "New" badge on This week, and a Mac notification, when a new digest arrives.
- **First login.** An account without a password yet is pointed to "Choose a password" as soon as the
  email address is filled in.
- **Company reading.** Companies with many pages no longer get their answer cut off.
- English copy review of every screen.
